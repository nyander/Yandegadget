## Pounds in, cedis out

You buy in the UK, so each item's **cost** and **list price** are in pounds. Customers and staff see cedis, worked out with the exchange rate.

Example: an item listed at £100, with a rate of 15, shows as **GH₵1,500**.

## Changing the rate

Go to **Settings → Exchange rates** and add a new rate. Don't edit old ones: the app keeps every rate, so it can show what anything was worth on any day.

When you add a new rate:

- website and staff prices change straight away;
- past sales don't change. Each sale saved the rate it was sold at.

## The lowest price in store

Staff can't sell below a percentage of the listed cedi price, set under **Shop settings**. It's 70% to start with, the same as in 2020.

## Changing one item's price

Open the item under **Catalogue → Stock units** and change its **List price** (in pounds).
