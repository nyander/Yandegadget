This app runs the shop's records and the website together. You don't need to be good with computers to use it: most days you'll use three or four screens.

## The big picture

1. **You buy items in the UK.** Each item you buy is recorded once, with its condition, what it cost in pounds and the price you want for it.
2. **You ship them to Accra.** You put items on a shipment and dispatch it. Staff in the store are told it's coming.
3. **Staff check the items in.** As each box is opened, staff tick off every item and take photos. Items marked as arrived appear on the website straight away.
4. **Customers see what's in the store.** The website shows prices in cedis at your exchange rate. Nothing is sold online: customers come in and agree the price with you.
5. **Staff record each sale.** They can't go below the lowest price you allow.
6. **Customers can ask you to find something.** They pay a deposit online, you look for it, and the deposit comes off the price when they buy it.
7. **The reports add it all up.** Sales, what the items cost, shipping, wages, rent and so on, so you can see real profit.

## Two places, one system

- **The admin panel** (this page, at `/admin`) is for you. Everything about stock, money, people and settings is here.
- **The staff screens** (at `/staff`) are for the people in the store: checking in shipments and selling. They work on a tablet or phone.

Customers only ever see the website.

## Help on every page

The first time you open a page, a short tour points out its parts, one pop-up at a time. Use **Next** and **Back** to move through it, or **Skip tour** to stop. The tour follows what you do: if you open a panel, for example the details behind a row on the dashboard, the tour explains that panel, then carries on where you left off when you close it.

To see a tour again, or to turn the tours off, click the **lifebuoy** button at the top right, next to the bell. It also opens this guide.

## Where to start

Work through [Getting set up](help:first-week). The checklist on your dashboard shows what's left to do.

## Pages keep themselves up to date

You don't need to refresh. If someone else adds to the buying list, a customer pays a deposit, or staff check in a shipment, it appears on your screen by itself within a few seconds. The dashboard figures update every 30 seconds and the bell every 10. Anything you're typing is left alone.
