Sales are recorded by staff on the **Sell** screen, not in the admin panel. That keeps the price negotiation in the store.

## What staff see

When staff find an item, they see:

- the **listed price** in cedis, at today's rate;
- the **lowest price they can accept**, which is a percentage of the listed price you set under **Shop settings** (70% to start with).

They can't record a sale below the lowest price. If a customer wants to go lower, they need to ask you. Either agree a higher price, or lower the item's list price first.

If the item is **reserved** for a customer who paid a deposit, the screen says so and takes the deposit off what they pay.

## What's saved

Each sale keeps the price paid, the listed price, the lowest price and the exchange rate on the day. Changing the rate later never changes past sales or profits.

## Mistakes

Sales can't be edited or deleted, so the records stay honest. If a sale was wrong, go to **Sales → Sales**, find it and use **Void**, with a reason. The item goes back into the store. Then staff record it again correctly.
