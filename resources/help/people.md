## Roles

Everyone who signs in has a role:

| Role     | Who                                | Can                              |
| -------- | ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| admin    | You                                | Everything, including this panel |
| staff    | People in the store                | Check in shipments, sell         |
| customer | Anyone who signs up on the website | Request items, pay deposits      |
| supplier | UK sellers                         | Offer you items                  |

## Adding staff or suppliers

Go to **Settings → Users → Add a person**. Enter their name and email, choose the role and, for staff, their store. Then choose how they get their password:

- **Email them a link to choose their own** — the best way. You never need to know their password.
- **Set one now and tell them** — for staff who don't easily get email. Type a password, or click **Generate** for a strong one that's easy to read out (like `Kp7m-Xq3r-Tz9w`). Click the copy button, then give it to them in person or by phone, never by email or text message.

## Passwords

Open someone under **Settings → Users**:

- **Email a password link** — sends them the same email as "Forgot password". Their old password keeps working until they choose a new one.
- **Set password** — type or **Generate** a new password and tell them it. They're signed out on their other devices and get an email saying the shop changed their password (the email doesn't include it).

Anyone can change their own password later under **Settings → Password** on the main site. To change yours, use that page too, not **Set password**.

Customers sign themselves up on the website.

## Someone has left

Open their account and remove their roles, so they can't do anything when they sign in. People are never deleted, so their past sales still show who made them.

## A customer asks you to delete their data

Open their account and use **Close account**. Their name, email and sign-in are removed for good. Their past sales and deposits stay in your records, without their name. The account then disappears from the **Users** list; to see closed accounts, use the filter and choose **Only closed accounts**. Accounts are closed rather than deleted so your sales and deposit records still add up. The privacy policy promises a reply within 21 days.

## Keeping accounts safe

- Ask staff to turn on two-factor sign-in under **Settings → Security** on the main site (`/settings/security`).
- Never share one login between people. Each sale records who made it.
- Forgotten password? Use **Forgot password** on the sign-in page.
