## Products and items

The catalogue has two levels:

- A **product** is what something is: "iPhone 13 128GB", with its photos and description. Customers see products on the website.
- A **stock unit** (an _item_) is one physical thing you own: this particular iPhone 13, in grade B, that cost £250, listed at £340. Each item has its own reference, like `YG-000123`.

So if you buy three iPhone 13s, that's one product with three items, each with its own condition and price.

## Adding what you've bought

1. Go to **Catalogue → Products**. Search for the product. If it isn't there, click **New product** and fill in the name, category, description and photos.
2. Open the product and scroll to **Stock units**. Click **Add unit**.
3. Fill in:
    - **Condition** — the grade.
    - **Cost** — what you paid, in pounds.
    - **List price** — what you want for it, in pounds. Customers see this in cedis.
    - **Serial / IMEI** — if it has one. It helps staff match the item when it arrives.
    - **Bought on** — the date you bought it.
    - **Starting status** — _Purchased (UK)_ for something still in the UK, or _In store_ for something already in Accra.
4. Add photos of this exact item if it has marks or wear.

## Where items are

Every item has a status:

| Status                 | Means                                  |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Purchased (UK)         | Bought, not shipped yet                |
| In transit             | On a shipment to Accra                 |
| In store               | In Accra and on the website            |
| Reserved               | Held for a customer who paid a deposit |
| Sold                   | Sold in store                          |
| Returned / Written off | Came back, or lost or broken           |

Items only appear on the website when they're **In store** or **Reserved**, and only if their product is **Published**.

## If something is lost or broken

Find the item under **Catalogue → Stock units** and use **Move** on its row to mark it _Written off_. It leaves your stock value.
