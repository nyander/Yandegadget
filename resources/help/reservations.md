Customers can reserve an item that's **in the store** on the website and pay for it straight away by **mobile money** (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AirtelTigo Money) or card. You keep it aside and they collect it.

## How it works for the customer

1. On an item in the shop, they click **Reserve and pay**.
2. They choose:
    - **Pay in full**: the listed cedi price. Nothing to pay when they collect.
    - **Pay a deposit**: a share of the price (20% to start with). They pay the rest in store, where the final price can still be agreed.
3. They pay by mobile money or card. The item is held for them for 30 minutes while they pay. If they don't finish, it goes back on sale by itself.
4. Once the payment goes through, the item is **Reserved** for them. Nobody else can reserve or buy it. They get an email saying when to collect it by (3 days to start with).

## What you do

- **Sales → Online reservations** lists everything waiting to be collected, with what they've paid and what's left. The number in the menu is how many are waiting. Staff and you get a notification for each new one.
- **Keep the item aside.** Its status shows Reserved everywhere.
- **When they come in**, open the item on the **Sell** screen (or **Sell to them** on the reservation). It says what they've paid online:
    - Paid in full: the price is filled in; just record the sale and hand it over.
    - Deposit: agree the price as usual (it can't be lower than the deposit or the lowest allowed price). Collect the difference.

## If they don't come, or cancel

| What happened                                         | Refund                                                           |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Not collected by the date                             | Everything they paid, automatically. The item goes back on sale. |
| The customer cancels on their reservation page        | Everything they paid                                             |
| You cancel (**Cancel and refund** on the reservation) | Everything they paid. Give a reason; they see it in the email.   |

Refunds go back to the mobile money wallet or card they paid with.

## Settings

Under **Shop settings → Reserving online**: the **deposit** (as a % of the price) and the **days to collect**. Changes apply to new reservations.

## Bulk items

For things you bought several of (see [Adding stock you've bought](help:stock)), the shop shows them as one line, like "Apple Watch · 47 in stock". Reserving picks any free one of them.

## Trying it without real money

On your own computer, payments go through a **test checkout** that looks like mobile money but moves no money: approve or decline the payment to see both outcomes. The live site uses Paystack.
