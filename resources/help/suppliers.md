UK sellers you work with can have a **supplier** account. They fill in their business details, then offer you items with photos and an asking price.

## Reviewing offers

New offers appear under **Buying → Supplier offers**, and you get a notification.

- **Buy** — you've agreed to buy it. Enter the **agreed price** (what you pay) and the **list price** for the shop, and pick the catalogue product it belongs to. The item is added to your stock as _Purchased (UK)_ with the supplier's photos, ready to put on a shipment.
- **Decline** — you don't want it. Give a reason; the supplier sees it.

The supplier sees the result on their offers page.

## Supplier records

**Buying → Suppliers** lists every business you buy from, with or without an account. Choosing a supplier on each item lets you see later where your best stock came from.

To give a supplier a login, add them under **Settings → Users** with the **supplier** role.
