When a customer wants something you don't have, they can ask you to find it on the website.

## How a request works

1. **The customer asks** for an item: what it is, the category and the lowest condition they'll accept.
2. **They pay a deposit** online with mobile money or card. The amount depends on the condition. You set it per grade under **Catalogue → Conditions**.
3. **You look for it.** Paid requests appear under **Sales → Customer requests**, and you get a notification.
4. **You match an item.** When you've bought one, use **Match an item** on the request and pick it. If it's already in the store, it's reserved straight away. If it's still in the UK, it's reserved when staff check it in.
5. **The customer is told** it's ready and has a number of days to collect it.
6. **They buy it in store.** Staff sell the reserved item; the deposit comes off the price.

The customer gets an email at each step.

## Cancelling and refunds

Refunds go back to the card or mobile money wallet the customer paid with.

| What happened                                              | Refund                                                         |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The customer cancels **before you've found** the item      | All of the deposit                                             |
| **You** cancel: you can't get it, or it isn't as described | All of the deposit                                             |
| The customer cancels **after you've found** it             | Deposit minus the cancellation fee                             |
| The customer **doesn't collect it** in time                | Deposit minus the cancellation fee; the item goes back on sale |

You set the cancellation fee (25% to start with) and the days to collect (14 to start with) under **Shop settings**. Uncollected items are released automatically each morning.

When you cancel a request yourself, choose **who is cancelling**. The screen shows the refund for each choice before you confirm. Customers can also cancel from their own request page, and they see the refund first.

Kept fees appear in the income statement as **Kept deposits**.
