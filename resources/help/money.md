The reports work most things out for you. You only type in what the app can't know.

## What the app already counts

You never need to enter these:

- sales, and what each sold item cost you;
- shipping costs (from each shipment);
- the value of your stock;
- deposits customers have paid and that you're holding for them.

## The ledger: everything else

Go to **Finance → Ledger → New entry** for anything else: rent, electricity, transport, cash in the bank, money you put into the business, loans, equipment.

Choose a **type** (for example _Rent_ or _Cash_), the date, a description and the amount in cedis or pounds. Use a **minus** amount to reduce a balance, for example paying back part of a loan.

Need a type that isn't there, like _Electricity_? Add it under **Finance → Ledger types**.

## Staff wages

Under **Finance → Staff wages**, add each person's wage and whether it's per **day**, **week** or **month**. The reports share it out over whatever dates you look at.

When a wage changes, give the old one an **end date**, then add the new one.

## The reports

Under **Reports**:

- **Income statement** — for the dates you choose: sales, minus what the items cost, gives your **gross profit**. Then minus shipping, wages and expenses gives your **net profit**.
- **Balance sheet** — on a given day: what the business owns (cash, stock, equipment) and owes (loans, deposits held).

Not sure what a line means? Hover over the **?** next to it (or tap it on a tablet). It says what the line is, where the number comes from, and why it matters. The dashboard figures have one too.

Negative numbers are **red, in brackets**. Both download as **PDF** or **CSV** (for Excel).

The balance sheet only adds up once your opening balances are in the ledger. Until then it shows a line called **Not yet explained by the ledger**.
