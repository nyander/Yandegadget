The dashboard shows what's selling. Each row in its tables opens the details behind it, so you can decide what to buy on your next trip to the UK, and keep a list.

## What sold: the details behind a row

On the **Dashboard**, the **What sold** box shows your sales for the dates at the top, one table at a time:

- **By category**, **By condition** or **By store**: pick one with the tabs.
- **Narrow it down** with the boxes on the right, for example _Phones_ only, to see which grades of phone sell best. _Show everything_ takes the filters off.
- **Sort by** sales, profit, margin, items sold or name. The button next to it switches between highest first and lowest first. You can also click a column heading.

**Click any row**, for example _Phones_ or _Mint_, and a panel opens on the right. If you've narrowed the table down, the panel only counts those sales, so _Mint_ with _Phones_ chosen shows only mint phones. The panel has:

- **A suggestion** at the top: _Worth buying more_, _Buy carefully_ or _Hold off for now_, with the reason.
- **The figures**: items sold, sales, profit and margin, profit per item, how many days items took to sell after arriving, how many you sell a month, and how many are in the store, on the way from the UK, or already on your buying list.
- **Best sellers**: the items that sold most, with what you paid for them in the UK on average.
- **Latest sales**: each sale, when it sold, what the customer paid and your profit.

### How the suggestion works

The app aims for about **two months of stock** at the pace you're selling, counting what's in the store and what's on the way. Shipping takes weeks, so two months stops you running out.

- **Worth buying more**: you'll run short. It tells you roughly how many to buy.
- **Hold off for now**: you already have enough for a while.
- **Buy carefully**: the margin is under 15%. Only buy more if you can pay less in the UK than last time.

It's a guide, not a rule. You know things the numbers don't, like a new model coming out.

## Your buying list

In the details panel, **Add to buying list** opens a short form, already filled in with the best seller, its category and condition, a suggested quantity and what you paid last time. Change anything, then **Add to list**.

You'll find the list under **Buying → Buying list** ([open it](admin:buying-list)). The number next to it is how many items are still to buy.

- **Bought**: tick it off once you've bought it. Then add the item to your stock as usual ([Adding stock you've bought](help:stock)).
- **Not needed**: you've changed your mind.
- **Add an item**: for anything you think of yourself.

The list starts on _To buy_. Change the filter to see what you've bought before.

## Stock age: what's sitting in the store

The **Stock age** box shows how long items have been in the store. **Click a row** to see those items, oldest first, with what they cost and their listed price. Click an item to open it.

Items over 60 or 90 days are a sign to check the price, and not to buy more of the same until they sell.
