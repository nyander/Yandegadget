Go to **Settings → Shop settings**. Under each setting, a box shows what it means in cedis or dates as you type, and warns you if a number looks risky. Nothing changes until you click **Save settings**; **Undo changes** puts back what's saved.

## Lowest price staff can accept

A percentage of the listed cedi price. Staff can't record a sale below it. 70% to start with, as in 2020. Lower it to give staff more room to negotiate; raise it to protect your margin.

## Cancellation fee once the item is found

When a customer cancels a request **after** you've found their item, or doesn't collect it in time, you keep this share of their deposit and refund the rest. 25% to start with. Set it to 0% to always refund everything.

Cancelling **before** you've found the item, or when **you** cancel, always refunds the whole deposit.

## Days to collect a reserved item

How long an item stays reserved once it's in the store. After that it goes back on sale and the cancellation fee applies. 14 days to start with. Customers see the date on their request and in their email.

## Remind the customer before the deadline

Customers get an email (and a note on their account) this many days before their reserved item goes back on sale. 3 days to start with. Each customer is reminded once. Set it to 0 to send no reminders. It has to be fewer days than the days to collect.

The website's terms page updates itself when you change these.

## Reserving online

**Deposit when paying part online** (20% to start with) and **Days to collect** (3 to start with) for items customers reserve and pay for on the website. Not collected in time means a full refund. See [Selling online: reservations](help:reservations).

## Change history

At the bottom of the page: every change, who made it, when, and what it was before. Useful if a price or refund ever looks wrong.

## Other things you can change

- **Exchange rates** — Settings → Exchange rates
- **Condition grades and deposits** — Catalogue → Conditions
- **Categories** shown on the website — Catalogue → Categories
