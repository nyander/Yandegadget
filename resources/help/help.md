## Common problems

**Prices on the website are in pounds.**
No exchange rate has been set. Add one under **Settings → Exchange rates**.

**An item isn't on the website.**
Check that the item is _In store_ (or _Reserved_), that its product is **Published**, and that its category is active.

**Staff can't record a sale.**
The price is below the lowest allowed price. Agree a higher price, or lower the item's list price.

**A customer says they paid a deposit but the request still says "Awaiting deposit".**
Look under **Sales → Online payments**. If the payment isn't there or failed, the money didn't go through; they can try again from their request page.

**Someone can't sign in.**
Ask them to use **Forgot password**. If they never got their invitation email, check their email address under **Settings → Users**.

**I made a mistake with a sale.**
**Void** it under **Sales → Sales**, then record it again.

## Your data is safe

The whole database and all photos are backed up every night, with copies kept for months. If anything is ever deleted by mistake, it can be brought back from a backup.

## Getting help

For anything the app does that you don't expect, contact Richard with:

- what you were trying to do;
- the page you were on (the web address at the top of the browser);
- a photo or screenshot of the screen.
