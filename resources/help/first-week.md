Before customers use the site, a few things need to be filled in. The **Getting started** checklist on the [dashboard](admin:) ticks each one off as you do it.

## 1. Set today's exchange rate

Go to **Settings → Exchange rates** and add today's rate from pounds to cedis. Every cedi price on the website comes from this. Without it, prices show in pounds. See [Prices and the exchange rate](help:prices).

## 2. Check the deposit for each condition

Go to **Catalogue → Conditions**. Each grade (A, B, C…) has the deposit a customer pays to request an item in that condition. They're still the 2020 amounts, so change them to what you want now. See [Customer requests and deposits](help:requests).

## 3. Check the refund rules

Go to **Settings → Shop settings**. Check the cancellation fee and how many days customers have to collect a reserved item. See [Shop settings](help:settings).

## 4. Add your staff

Go to **Settings → Users → Add a person**. Give them the **staff** role and choose their store. They get an email to set their own password. See [Staff and customer accounts](help:people).

## 5. Protect your own account

Your account can see all the money, so turn on two-factor sign-in. On the main site, open **Settings → Security** (the address is `/settings/security`).

## 6. Enter what's already in the store

Add each item you already have in Accra, with the starting status **In store**, and a photo if you can. See [Adding stock you've bought](help:stock).

## 7. Enter your opening balances and wages

So the reports start from the right place: cash in the bank, money you put into the business, any loans, and each person's wage. See [Money: ledger, wages and reports](help:money).

## Then try it for a week

Sell through the staff screens alongside your notebook for a week or two. Compare them each evening. When they agree, you can trust the system and share the website.
